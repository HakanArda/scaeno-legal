Support

Last updated: August 28, 2026

If you need help with Scaeno, email support and describe the issue you encountered. Scaeno is developed and operated by Hakan Arda Güven.

Contact support: trustsoftwareapp@gmail.com

What to include

  • The email address or username on your Scaeno account, if you have one.
  • Your device type and operating system, such as iPhone, iPad, or Android.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected to happen.
  • Any error message shown in the app.

Common support topics

  • Account access, sign-in, email verification, or password reset issues.
  • Problems with gameplay, scores, streaks, achievements, or leaderboards.
  • Profile, username, or avatar issues.
  • Privacy, account deletion, or data questions.

For account and data deletion instructions, visit Account & Data Deletion.

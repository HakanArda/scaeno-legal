Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: July 22, 2026

You can permanently delete your Scaeno account and all associated data at any time.

In the app

Open Scaeno, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account, and confirm. This immediately and irreversibly deletes your account and everything tied to it.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile and username
  • Your email address and login credentials
  • Your quiz session and round history
  • Your achievements and streak progress
  • Your uploaded profile photo
  • Your leaderboard entries

Can't access the app?

Email trustsoftwareapp@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, and we'll process the deletion within 7 days.
